# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2025

## Overview

anymodel is a client-side AI chat interface. Your data never leaves your browser.

## Data Storage

- **API keys** are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your passphrase and stored in your browser's localStorage. They are never sent to our servers (we have no servers).
- **Conversations** are stored in your browser's localStorage. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the AI provider you choose.
- **Settings** (theme, model selection, system prompt) are stored locally in your browser.

## Data Transmission

- Your messages are sent **only** to the AI provider you select (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google).
- Your API key is sent **only** to the provider it belongs to.
- No data is sent to any third party, analytics service, or our servers.

## Analytics

We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io/), a privacy-respecting analytics service. Plausible:
- Does not use cookies
- Does not collect personal data
- Counts page views and basic interactions only
- Is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy

## What We Don't Do

- We don't have access to your API keys
- We don't read your conversations
- We don't sell or share your data
- We don't use tracking cookies
- We don't have user accounts or registration

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/anymodel/anymodel).
